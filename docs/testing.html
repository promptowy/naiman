Playback measurements

Original and 2x on one RTX 4090

We replayed the same 30-second GTA VI recording from Xbox Remote Play twice on Windows. NAIMAN captured a 1280 × 720 player window. A second recorder sampled its 640 × 360 output up to 120 times per second.

Original playback: 25.1 visible changes/s across 27 seconds of clear motion. NAIMAN 2x: 46.7 across 25 seconds of clear motion. These are measurements from the first preview of the renderer.

We counted changes in the centre after downsampling to 256 × 144 grayscale. A change counted when the mean absolute brightness difference exceeded 0.45 on a 0-255 scale and over 3% of pixels changed by at least three levels. We averaged seconds with at least eight clear changes. Compression, slight motion and capture timing affect this estimate.

What we verified

The 2x engine ran on a 2560 × 1440 monitor. The built-in 30 Hz scene, both setup paths, original/2x switching and exit were checked on Windows. The Xbox window selector also recognized a local replay in Edge. A physical PS5, HDMI hardware and controller-to-screen latency await separate tests.

Po polsku

Na RTX 4090 dwukrotne odtworzenie tego samego klipu dało w ruchu średnio 25,1 widocznej zmiany/s w trybie oryginalnym i 46,7 z interpolacją. To wynik nagrania odtwarzanego na PC. Uczciwe porównanie z Veyrą, SmoovPlay i NitLinkiem wymaga wspólnej metody i pomiaru opóźnienia sterowania.
